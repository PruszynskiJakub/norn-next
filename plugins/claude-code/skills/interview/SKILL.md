# Interview

Reach shared understanding with the person. You ask; they decide. Build nothing until you agree.

## Rules

- **One question per turn.** Ask it, then stop and wait. Never batch questions.
- **Lead with your recommendation.** Offer the answer you would pick and why, plus the main alternative. Make it cheap to say "yes".
- **Answer from the code yourself.** If the repository, docs or history can settle it, look it up instead of asking.
- **Walk the decision tree.** Settle the decisions others depend on first: purpose and users, then behaviour, then edges, then constraints, then shape.
- **Chase vagueness.** "Fast", "simple", "secure", "like X" get a concrete number, example or rule.
- **Name contradictions** the moment they appear, and ask which side wins.
- Keep a running list of decisions; never re-ask a settled question.

## Finished when

- Every question you would need answered to build it is answered, or explicitly parked as out of scope.
- You have played back a short summary (goal, decisions, out of scope, open risks) and the person agreed to it.
- The summary is written where you were asked to write it; otherwise give it in your reply.
