# 02.AC5 — docs/limitations.md: under record.policy local a crash still loses receipts/counters since last record push; step restarts at that boundary with fresh counters
docs/limitations.md:28 (exact):
"Under the `local` record policy, a holder that crashes without releasing its claim still loses the receipts and counters in the run record since its last record push (the last release, or the last time `open-pr` opened or updated the PR). Its work does not go: every boundary also pushes the story's head to `refs/norn/heads/<story>`, under either policy, and a takeover resumes the worktree from that ref, merged with the remote story branch (never rebased). The step restarts at the boundary of that last record push, with the counters it recorded; a story whose record was never pushed restarts the step the heads ref's last boundary led to, with fresh counters. `record.policy: remote` pushes the record whenever it changes instead."
Observed vs criterion: loss of receipts and counters and restart at the last-record-push boundary are stated. "Fresh counters" applies only to a story with no pushed record; otherwise "the counters it recorded" — the handoff says this matches the code, and it is the more accurate wording.
Caveat: "merged with the remote story branch" is not true when the heads ref and branch diverge (see 02.AC3 repro); the doc overstates that.
Verdict: pass (with the caveat for the person).
