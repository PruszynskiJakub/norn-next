# 03.AC4 worktrees follow the bound home
- NORN_HOME_DIR (harness default): worktree under that home's repos/<id>/worktrees (observe-output.md).
- Home given to the command (deps.home, no NORN_HOME_DIR): tick exit 0, worktree at `<dir>/home-c/repos/repo-c-<id>/worktrees/gh-10-two-places` = expected prefix (observe2-output.md).
- Symlinked NORN_HOME_DIR: worktree under the resolved real home (observe3-output.md).
There is no `--home` CLI flag (tick --home exits 2, usage); the spec's --home means the home given to the command, as recorded in the handoff.
