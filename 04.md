## AC5 — every graph draws without a Mermaid error — PASS (rendered locally with mermaid-cli, not on GitHub itself)

How: pulled the 7 ```` ```mermaid ```` blocks out of `docs/workflows.md` into `g1..g7.mmd`, and rendered each with `npx -y @mermaid-js/mermaid-cli@11.4.2` (mermaid 11.4, the major GitHub uses), pointed at the installed Chrome through a puppeteer config.
